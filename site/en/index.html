Problem

Small roll anomalies
become product defects.

In roll-to-roll processes, guide roll degradation and rotation anomalies are hard to notice, yet they affect the quality of the entire process.

01

Guide roll degradation & rotation anomalies

As bearings and rolls wear over long operating hours, rotation becomes unstable.

→
02

Tension imbalance

Slip, idle spinning and vibration make the tension applied to the film uneven.

→
03

Scratches & quality defects

This leads to scratches, wrinkles and other defects that cost quality and productivity.

Our Approach

Evolvix detects problems before defects occur.

  • Early detection of rotation & vibration anomalies
  • Assess guide roll degradation in advance
  • Predictive maintenance at the right time
  • Prevent defects and unplanned downtime
Product

RMS-ON

A wireless predictive maintenance solution for roll-to-roll processes — a smart monitoring platform that wirelessly collects RPM and vibration data from hundreds of guide rolls and detects early signs of anomalies.

System

One wireless system, from sensor node to server

RMS-ON Edge Zigbee → RMS-ON Gateway Ethernet / Wi-Fi → On-premise Server
Product 01

RMS-ON Edge

A battery-powered wireless sensor node that integrates a non-contact optical RPM sensor and a 3-axis MEMS accelerometer. With Zigbee/XBee communication and a TDMA collection scheme, it periodically collects condition data from many guide rolls and switches to a power-saving state after each measurement for long-term operation.

Product 02

RMS-ON Gateway

A data relay device that collects Zigbee sensing data from multiple RMS-ON Edge end nodes and connects to an on-premise server over Ethernet or Wi-Fi. Status LEDs and an external antenna make network status easy to check on site.
