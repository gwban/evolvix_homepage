01 / Rotation and vibration

Beyond measuring,
we look for warning signs.

Rotation changes are checked together with left and right vibration at the same moment.

02 / Warning signs

What changed,
told apart clearly.

  1. 01
    Guide roll degradationSigns of wear in accumulated vibration changes.
  2. 02
    Left–right imbalanceDifferences in left and right vibration size and pattern at the same moment.
  3. 03
    Signs of slipMoments when rotation speed changes abruptly.
  4. 04
    Impact eventsShort, large vibration changes and when they happened.

Warning signs are information to support maintenance decisions. What is provided may vary with the installation and what we agree on.

03 / Speed changes

Normal speed changes,
told apart from momentary anomalies.

Acceleration and deceleration are shown as normal operation;
abrupt momentary changes are flagged as separate events.

  1. Measuring point
  2. Speed signal
  3. Anomaly

04 / Left and right vibration

Differences between sides,
down to momentary impacts.

Compare left and right vibration at the same moment,
and see exactly when a sudden change happened.

05 / Monitoring

Which roll changed,
and when.

See the condition of dozens of rolls at a glance,
then look into the speed and vibration of the one you select.
