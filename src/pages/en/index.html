Machine vision inspection

Differences you can't see,
turned into data you can measure.

On uniform surfaces like optical film or copper foil, a single small mark is a defect. We set up lighting and cameras for the material to capture differences the eye barely sees.

Locate tiny defects
and see their shape.

Every defect found is logged with its position, size and shape, and sorted by type: scratch, white spot, particle and so on.

RMS-ON · Process monitoring

Every guide roll,
at a glance.

RMS-ON Edge mounts like a module on the fixed support that holds the roll. It is a battery-powered wireless node, so no cabling work is needed.

Rotation and vibration
show how equipment changes.

A non-contact optical sensor measures RPM and a 3-axis MEMS accelerometer measures vibration. Readings are collected at the gateway over Zigbee.

From readings
to maintenance decisions.

When a roll starts behaving differently from normal, it is flagged right away, so you can see which roll to check first.
