How we work

{{icon:flask}}

We test first
and show you the results.

Instead of long explanations, we check with your samples and your line data first. You can decide once you've seen the results.

{{icon:factory}}

We don't force
a fixed setup onto your line.

Different materials, defects and line speeds call for different lighting, cameras and algorithms. We build to fit the process.

{{icon:wrench}}

Installation is
where the work starts.

After rollout, we keep reviewing the data with you and adjusting inspection criteria and models.

{{icon:shield}}

We keep
downtime short.

Like our wireless sensors that need no cabling work, we look first for ways that don't require major changes to your equipment.
